## The setup

Me and my friends Radhasyam and Sujitha went to watch Dhurandhar 2 at
Airia Mall — the same place we watched Dhurandhar 1. Felt right to
complete it where we started.

## The movie

It's a 4-hour spy thriller. That runtime sounds exhausting on paper,
but it genuinely kept us on edge through most of it. The pacing doesn't
drag the way you'd expect a 4-hour film to.

Ranveer Singh as Jaskirat Singh is the standout. He disappears into the
role completely — there's a physicality and intensity to the performance
that makes you forget you're watching Ranveer Singh. The music holds up
the tension in all the right moments.

## The climax

Ends on an emotional note. Won't say more. But the three of us sat
quietly for a moment after the credits started — that kind of ending.

## Worth it?

Yes. Go with people you watch movies with, not just anyone.
The kind of film that's better shared.
