## What is this book?

The Almanack of Naval Ravikant is not written by Naval — it was compiled
by Eric Jorgenson from Naval's tweets, podcasts, and interviews over the years.
That detail matters because it reads differently from a regular book.
It's dense with ideas, not narrative.

## How I read it

Took me 2 months. Not because it's long — it isn't. Because I kept stopping
to think. Some pages I read three times.

## Points that stayed with me

- Wealth is created by building things that scale while you sleep.
- Happiness is a skill, not a trait. You can get better at it deliberately.
- Specific knowledge — what only you can do — is where real leverage lives.
- Most of our suffering comes from wanting things to be different
  from what they are.

## Would I recommend it?

Yes, but don't rush it. Read 10 pages, close it, sit with it.
It rewards slow reading more than most books I've picked up.
